# prathish.com

Independent research on enterprise software economics — what organisations actually pay, what
they actually buy, and whether the forecasts hold up.

Built from public sources: published contract notices, regulatory filings, job-posting data and
vendor documentation.

## Forecast registry

Dated, falsifiable forecasts live at [prathish.com/registry/](https://prathish.com/registry/).
Each carries a confidence level, a leading indicator anyone can check, and a resolution date.

Resolved forecasts stay published whether they landed or not. **The commit history of this
repository is the dated record** — entries are not edited after publication, and corrections
appear as new rows rather than quiet revisions.

## Method

- Every material claim carries a dated source
- Verified facts are distinguished from inference, and say which they are
- Forecasts are falsifiable and carry a stated leading indicator
- Any commercial relationship relevant to a piece is disclosed in that piece
