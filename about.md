I'm Prathish Kumar Srinivasan, an engineering leader in Melbourne, Australia.

Nineteen years in banking technology, and five fairly different jobs inside it: operating
internet banking systems in 24x7 environments and acting as recovery lead on severe incidents,
then technical analysis, then leading complex releases across ten-plus customer channels, then
owning delivery and production stability of a mobile banking backend platform, and now leading
technology leaders across several squads. Along the way, fifty-plus Go microservices, distributed
teams across three countries, and a long education in why production systems fail.

Technology has never been only the day job. I build and maintain open-source tools in Go and
Linux, mostly around the security of AI agents — see [projects](/projects/). One of them turned
up a high-severity vulnerability in an upstream project, which remains the most instructive thing
I have done outside work.

## What you'll find here

- Engineering: Go, Linux and container internals, APIs, platform design
- Security: application and API security, AI-agent security, vulnerability management
- Reliability: incidents, recovery, operational resilience, and what regulation actually asks for
- Investigations: what public records show about enterprise technology spending and renewals
- Opinions, and forecasts I am willing to be scored on

Some of it is hands-on. Some is analysis of public data. I try to keep the difference visible —
where something is inferred rather than established, it says so, and I would rather revisit a
conclusion than defend it.

## How I work

I try to keep established facts and my own inference visibly apart, and where something is
inferred it says so. Claims carry a dated source, the primary one where it exists. Forecasts are
falsifiable, dated, and carry a leading indicator; resolved ones stay published whether they
landed or not, and corrections appear as new entries rather than quiet edits.

AI tools help me find sources, read them at volume, extract and structure data, and draft. What
happens next depends on the figure: one taken from a document is checked against that document, a
derived figure is recomputed, and a measurement taken on a given day is dated and published as a
measurement rather than a standing fact. The interpretations and the conclusions are mine.

## Transparency

Nothing here is sponsored, and no organisation has reviewed or approved a piece before
publication. Any commercial relationship relevant to a piece is disclosed in that piece, and
**no assessment is revised because of a commercial relationship — in either direction.**

Every change to this site is in its [public repository](https://github.com/prathish-ks/prathish-ks.github.io),
so the date on a forecast is verifiable rather than something you take on trust.
