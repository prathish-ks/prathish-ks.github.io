Independent research on enterprise software economics.

I look at what organisations actually pay for enterprise software, what they actually buy, and
whether the things people confidently predict about this market turn out to be true.

Every forecast I make is [published with a date, a confidence level and a falsification
trigger](/registry/) — and scored afterwards, including the ones I get wrong.
